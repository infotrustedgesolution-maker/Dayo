About this policy

Dayo is made by TrustEdge Solution. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is protected. By using Dayo you agree to this policy.

Data we collect

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile photo, used only to sign you in.
  • Business details you enter: business name, owner name, phone number and city.
  • Vehicle details: vehicle number, type, model, company, document dates, FASTag ID and notes.
  • Daily trip records: crew names and phone numbers, kilometers, location, diesel, toll, order IDs and amounts.
  • Staff details: name, email address, role and permissions set by the owner.

Data we do not collect

Dayo does not collect your GPS location, contacts, photos, messages or call logs.

Ads

Dayo shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information. In the EEA, UK and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How we use your data

Your data is used only to run the app: to save your daily records, calculate totals, create reports, and let the owner share access with their staff.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, India region) and in a local copy on your phone so the app works offline. Data is encrypted in transit.

Who can see your data

Only members of your business can see its data. The owner can see everything. Staff can see only the vehicles assigned to them, and amounts are hidden from staff unless the owner allows it. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Sharing reports

When you export a PDF or Excel report and share it (for example on WhatsApp or email), it is sent by you through the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy applies to the shared file.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your business account is active. The owner can delete vehicles, entries and staff at any time from the app. To delete your whole account and all business data, contact us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

Dayo is a business app and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy Policy.
